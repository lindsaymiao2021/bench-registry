NYC Parks · Van Cortlandt Park, Bronx

Bench Adoption Registry

Van Cortlandt Park's bench adoption program lets neighbors, families and local groups sponsor one of the park's 520 benches, often with a brass plaque honoring someone they love. This registry is the program's single record: it shows which benches are adopted, by whom, and until when, and it's where you reserve an open bench for yourself.

  1. Find a bench on the map or list
  2. Create a free account
  3. Choose your dates and plaque
Bench positions are approximate.
